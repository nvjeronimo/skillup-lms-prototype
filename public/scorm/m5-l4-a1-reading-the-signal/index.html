Reading the Signal

A macro judgment activity for financial analysts, from scenario data to sector positioning.

What you'll practice

By completing this activity, you will be able to classify scenario commentary as base, bull, or bear case using only the supplied data, and match sectors to the macro driver they are most sensitive to.

How this activity works

You'll take on a role, review the evidence exactly as it would appear in a real review, then make two judgment calls of your own.

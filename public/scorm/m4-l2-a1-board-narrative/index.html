From export to board narrative

A judgment activity for financial analysts using AI to draft board-ready commentary.

What you'll practice

By completing this activity, you will be able to separate AI-supported financial claims from unsupported ones, and prioritize verified movements for a board audience based on materiality, not size alone.

How this activity works

You'll take on a role, review the evidence exactly as it would appear in a real review, then make two judgment calls of your own.

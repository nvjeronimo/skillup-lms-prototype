Clearing the Deck

A disclosure review activity for AI-assisted investor communications.

What you'll practice

By completing this activity, you will be able to judge whether AI-assisted investor communications meet disclosure requirements, and decide the correct next action for the ones that don't.

How this activity works

You'll take on a role, review the disclosure criteria, then make two review calls of your own.

Your role

Disclosure reviewer, Larkspur Renewables

An analyst used generative AI to help draft the investor deck for your five-year revenue outlook. Before it goes out, you're reviewing each statement in the deck against your disclosure requirements, then deciding what happens to the ones that don't clear.

Criteria

What a statement has to clear

Accurate roleDescribes what AI actually did, without implying it validated or independently derived the forecast.
TraceableEvery material statement can be traced back to an approved source, not just polished language.
Material impactJudged by whether it could affect an investor's decision, not by whether AI touched it.
ReviewedConfirmed by finance for accuracy, and by legal or compliance for balance and obligations.

A statement can fail on any one of these even if the other three hold.

Before you clear it for release

Ask yourself these four questions before you sign off.

1

Does the disclosure describe what AI actually did, without implying it validated or independently produced the forecast?

2

Can every material statement be traced back to an approved source?

3

Has materiality been judged by potential impact on an investor's decision, not by whether AI touched the content?

4

Have finance, legal, and compliance completed a documented review before this goes out?
